Google Play Account Deletion Disclosure

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, users of Manifestation can permanently delete their player account and all associated cloud data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account using any of the following three methods:

  • Method 1 (In-Game): Launch the game, tap your Call-Sign in the top header or open ⚙️ Settings → Account Management, and click "PERMANENTLY DELETE ACCOUNT".
  • Method 2 (Online Form Below): Enter your registered Call-Sign and password into the verified self-service deletion form below.
  • Method 3 (Email Support): Send an email from your contact address to support@manifestationgame.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and your Call-Sign. Your account will be purged within 48 hours.

Types of Data Deleted & Retention Period

  • Data Deleted: Call-Sign (Username), salted password hash, total credits, inventory, unlocked operative skins, match stats (escapes/captures), and temporary server session tokens.
  • Retention Period: Zero days (immediate deletion). Once confirmed, all user records are permanently and irreversibly purged from our MongoDB database and server storage.
