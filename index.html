MISSION OBJECTIVES: HOW TO SURVIVE & ESCAPE
⚡ STEP 1
Restore Grid Power — Search corridors for yellow wall Circuit Breakers. Repair them to power up the Master Vault Gate and Keypad terminal! (1 required in Training/Solo, 3 in Multiplayer).
📝 STEP 2
Collect Cipher Clues — Inspect glowing white clue notes floating in corridor paths. Each note reveals digits of the 4-digit Master Vault security code.
🔢 STEP 3
Crack the Vault Keypad — Locate the Master Vault Keypad at the corridor terminus and enter the 4-digit code. Submitting the code decrypts the lock and reveals WHICH 2 Twin Extraction Keys are needed!
🔑 STEP 4
Retrieve & Insert Twin Keys — Search corridors for the 2 matching gemstone keys (e.g. Amber Key & Sapphire Key), carry them to the Master Vault, and insert each key into the gate sockets!
🌲 STEP 5
Master Gate Escape — Approach the massive Master Vault blast doors with power on and both keys installed. Press E / tap the blast door to escape into the pine forest!
TACTICAL SURVIVAL MECHANICS
🏮 LANTERNS
UV Light Sanctuaries — Warm brass ceiling lanterns mark safe havens. Standing in the golden light rapidly regenerates Sanity (+8.5%/s) and shields you from hallucinations!
🕯️ ARIADNE
Ariadne's Thread (Dev Protocol) — Glowing orbs floating along corridor paths mark safe navigation routes leading directly back to the Master Vault!
🗺️ MINIMAP
Minimap & Tactical Pings — Top-right radar displays sound pings and allies. Press M or tap radar to open full Tactical Map. Click anywhere on map to drop colored beacon pings!
📦 DROP ITEMS
Share Supplies & Keys — Press Q (or tap DROP ITEM) to drop your held item on the floor for teammates. Press G (or tap DROP KEY) to drop carried gate keys!
🧠 SANITY
Sanity & Hallucinations — Darkness and proximity to ghostly entities drain Sanity. Below 40% Sanity, you experience terrifying shadow hallucinations. Recover sanity in Light Sanctuaries or by reading lore notes!
