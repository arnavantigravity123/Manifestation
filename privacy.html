MANIFESTATION

Privacy Policy — Effective Date: September 2026

Google Play & RevenueCat Compliant

1. Overview

Welcome to Manifestation ("the Game"), developed and operated by Arnav. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data processed when you download, install, or play Manifestation on Android, iOS, or via the Web.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver authentic multiplayer gameplay, player authentication, and save state persistence:

  • Account Information: If you register an account, we store your chosen username and a securely hashed password (using cryptographic salting). We do not collect your real name, physical address, or phone number.
  • Game Progress & Stats: Match statistics, inventory status, unlocked cosmetics/skins, credits, and achievements.
  • Device & Network Information: Basic device telemetry (screen resolution, operating system, and WebSocket connection status) to optimize 3D rendering and maintain low-latency multiplayer synchronization.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (RevenueCat)

Manifestation offers optional in-app purchases, such as the VIP Pass and cosmetic items.

All financial transactions are handled directly by the official app stores (Google Play Billing / Apple App Store) and our in-app subscription infrastructure partner, RevenueCat. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking details.

For details on how RevenueCat processes purchase tokens to validate digital receipts, please consult the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game utilizes trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services: Used for authentication, app distribution, and purchase processing on Android.
  • RevenueCat: Used for managing purchase receipts, VIP subscriptions, and entitlements.
  • MongoDB: Secure cloud database used to persist player game accounts, scores, and inventory.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Manifestation does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Player accounts use non-identifiable usernames. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove the data.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated gameplay data at any time. To request account deletion, please contact us at the email below with your in-game username, and your records will be permanently purged from our database within 48 hours.

7. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information. We implement industry-standard cryptographic techniques (salted password hashing, encrypted HTTPS/WSS communication) to protect your account against unauthorized access or tampering.
